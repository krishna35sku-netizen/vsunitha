Academic Profile

Profile

Prof. Dr. V. Sunitha is a Professor in the Department of Geology at Yogi Vemana University, Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh, India.

Her academic career includes teaching, research, academic administration and supervision of research scholars.

Her research work focuses on Environmental Geochemistry, Hydro-geochemistry, Groundwater Pollution, Hydrogeology, Remote Sensing and Geographic Information Systems (GIS).

She served as a Raman Postdoctoral Fellow at the Department of Geology and Environmental Earth Sciences, Miami University, Oxford, Ohio, USA, during 2016–2017.
